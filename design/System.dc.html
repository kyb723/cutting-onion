Cutting Onions · Web design system

The kit

Everything on the site is built from the printed menu and the shopfront sign: fat display caps, 3–4px black rules, hard offset shadows, flat colour. No gradients, no soft drop shadows, no rounded-card blandness.

Marks

The crying face Primary. Black line art. On orange it is the avatar; on cream it is the sign. Never recoloured, never cropped past the disc.
Please don’t cry The onion in sunglasses with the knife. The hero is built out of it: every onion in the field is this character, round, legless, wordless.
The burger guy Combo and offer moments only. Solid orange on cream, solid black on orange.
